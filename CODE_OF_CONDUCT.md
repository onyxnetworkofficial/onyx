# Code of conduct

Onyx is built in the open by people who may never meet. These rules keep that work possible.

## Do

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Argue about evidence and code, not about people.
- Accept a review, and give one that says what to change and why.

## Do not

- Harass, insult or threaten anyone.
- Post anyone's personal data: names, addresses, e-mails, wallet owners. This also applies to
  data you submit (labels name organizations only, never people).
- Try to find out who a contributor is when they choose to stay anonymous.
- Spam issues, pull requests or the data network.

## Reporting

Open an issue if you are comfortable doing so in public. Otherwise use the repository's private
vulnerability reporting form (the **Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**) and start the
title with "Conduct". Only the maintainers see it. You can also report content to GitHub itself.

## What happens

The maintainers decide. They can edit or hide content, close a thread, or block an account from
the repository. They reply to the reporter within 7 days.
