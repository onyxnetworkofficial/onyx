Findings from this review: THREATS LC16-1 and LC16-2 (below and in THREATS.md); the concentration
on one key is NL6-5, NL6-12 and NL6-13.

- **LC16-1 (Low, TODO S4).** `OnyxReobserve`'s owner adds and removes observers at any time, with
  no bond and no delay; the set at a task's open decides every re-observed stake. With the deployer
  as owner, one key picks who decides.
- **LC16-2 (Low, TODO S3).** Panel seats are first come, capped at 256: one funded actor can take
  every free seat, decide fallbacks and lock others out (with the minter it costs nothing, NL6-13).
